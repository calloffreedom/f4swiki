Palo Alto Changeover

Network for Learning is changing their spyware solution from Fortinet to Palo Alto, meaning that students’ data will be fed into an AI learning model.

Why

It is believed that N4L wants tighter control over student internet access1 (more “centralised”). This will allow them to collect more student information and data, allowing them to train their AI models to recognise VPN traffic so it can be blocked.

Timeline

At the start of 2026, N4L started offering a new spyware certificate for use with Palo Alto.
